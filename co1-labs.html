The labs for this module live in two Colab notebooks: Theory & Practicals (lecture walkthroughs plus the graded lab blocks) and Tutorial (a code-along build of an invoice automation). Open them in Colab, work through the cells as the lectures progress. No installs, no setup — Colab runs Python 3 for you, free, in the browser.
Theory & Practicals — COI walkthroughs & labs  · COI-1 · COI-2 · COI-3
Walks through each COI with worked examples, then hands you the lab blocks — Lab 1.1 GST invoices & paise math, the code-trace clinic, and Lab 1.2 discount slabs & billing functions. Every YOUR TURN cell is yours to solve.

Tutorial — build an invoice automation  · code-along · auto-checked
Starts from one order and one invoice, grows it to all orders, a discount rule, invoices for every order and functions, and ends in a daily sales summary. Finish with the YOUR TURN multi-file case and let the auto-checker grade your function.
Open in Colab

Offline or Colab down? Download the file instead and open it with File → Open Notebook in Colab.

1

Open a notebook

Click Open in Colab on either card above. A free Google account is all you need — there is nothing to install. If Colab ever asks you to connect Drive, allow it; that is just Colab reaching the notebook.

2

Run cell by cell, in order

Click a cell and press ▶ Run (or Cmd/Ctrl + Enter). Walkthrough cells show the demo; when you reach a cell marked YOUR TURN, write the code yourself and run it before moving on.

3

Keep your record

Before you close, choose File → Save copy in Drive. Your saved copy with your answers is your lab record for the module.

WHAT IS INSIDE EACH FILE

  • Theory & Practicals — three COI walkthroughs: COI-1 business data types (dictionaries & lists of dicts), COI-2 data structures (lists, dictionaries, tuples & sets), COI-3 functions, clean code & automation — each followed by its lab block, all with YOUR TURN cells
  • Tutorial — one code-along project: invoice automation, from a single order to a discount rule to a daily sales summary, finishing with a multi-file case and an auto-checker cell that grades your function
Tip: the notebooks follow the lecture order, so you can work on them during the week — do a lab block right after its lecture, then the tutorial once you know functions. Both are plain Python 3, so they also open in any local IDE (VS Code, Jupyter) if you ever need to work without Google.
